# Poster brief for ChatGPT: Lyceum, A1 portrait

You are designing a printed poster for a judged booth. Read the whole brief before you start. The words, numbers and rules below are fixed; the design is yours.

## 1. What to make

- **The poster.** A1 portrait, 594 by 841 mm, for the Lyceum booth at the HACKaSTONE 2026 Grand Final (theme "Agentic AI for Education"), Amsterdam, 29 October 2026. Judges walk past it, then stop and read.
- **Visual first.** Every section leads with one picture: a diagram, a chart, an icon row, a mascot or a logo row. Words are short labels. A judge two metres away should get each section from its picture and its heading alone.
- **One story, top to bottom.** Four numbered parts tell it: **01 Valued** (the problem), **02 Valid** (the solution), **03 Valuable** (the market), **04 Valunique** (the difference). Between 02 and 03 comes the system architecture; after 04 come responsible AI and the ask. Arrows or a thin connecting line can lead the eye from part to part.
- **Deliver two files.**
  1. **A PNG** of the whole poster, portrait, at the highest resolution you can (aim for 3508 by 4961 pixels or more), for review and sharing.
  2. **A PowerPoint file (.pptx)** with one slide sized exactly 59.4 by 84.1 cm (A1 portrait), built with code (python-pptx):
     - every word as live, editable text, in the fonts named below;
     - the attached PNGs placed as pictures, never redrawn;
     - each section grouped, so the team can move or fix it.

     The team will open it in PowerPoint, check it, and export the print PDF from there.

  The PNG and the PPTX must show the same layout, text and images. If you can render the PPTX to PNG (for example with LibreOffice), do that; otherwise draw the PNG from the same layout. If one part cannot be built, say which, and give its measurements in centimetres.

**Type** (all free on Google Fonts):
- Bricolage Grotesque: headings and big numbers, bold.
- Plus Jakarta Sans: labels and short lines.
- JetBrains Mono: small capitals for eyebrows and chips.

Headings at least 60 pt and labels at least 18 pt, at A1 scale.

**Style:**
- Rounded cards with a 2 px forest outline on light cards; pill-shaped chips.
- Flat vector style, friendly and a little bold (think Duolingo with a slight brutalist edge).
- Forest bands carry a faint dot grid, like the attached banner.
- Simple line icons in forest or emerald.
- No stock photos, no 3D, no gradients, no people's faces.

**Style references** (attached if available): the team's banner (forest with a lime dot grid, mascots on cards); the team's earlier A1 poster (mint dotted background, rounded cards, forest header, numbered sections); the GreenWater AI deck (big numbered section blocks such as "01 Valued").

**Mascots:**
- Use only the attached PNGs, exactly as they are: no redrawing, no recolouring, no new poses.
- Tualang, the young tree, is Lyceum's host: it greets at the top and celebrates at the end.
- The seven helpers stand at their steps in the loop. Keep them small; they support the diagram and never dominate it.

**Logos:**
- Use only the attached PNGs, unchanged, all at the same height.
- Put each one on a white or tint tile with its product name in text under it and a status chip.
- Never draw, imitate or invent a logo.
- "ChatGPT Edu" has no logo file: write the name in a plain text tile.
- Competitors in the comparison table appear as names only, never logos.

## 3. Layout, top to bottom

The approximate share of the poster's height is in brackets. The exact words for every block are in Section 4.

1. **Header (11%)**, a forest band. The Lyceum mark and wordmark on the left, the headline beside them, the mono line above it, the four part chips under it. Tualang greeting on the right.
2. **01 Valued (15%).** Two separate graphics side by side, never merged into one chart:
   - a calendar strip of 18 month squares with 12 filled ("12 to 18 months");
   - a 10 by 10 waffle with 39 squares filled ("39%").

   Then a row of four field icons (biology, engineering, business, creative) under "AI is reaching every field". Then two Malaysia number tiles ("455" and "32.2%"). Then Tualang reflecting, with a speech bubble.
3. **02 Valid (24%)**, the hero section.
   - **The loop (left).** A large loop on a forest card: a lime track with arrows running clockwise through six stations. Each station has its mascot and a three to six word label; Decide has no mascot, only a people or scales icon in a lime circle. Folio sits in the centre with "Your course".
   - **The receipt (right).** A paper slip with zigzag edges, holding a real quotation, with a "Real posting" stamp and a red "Paraphrase refused" stamp beside it.
   - **The quote (under them).** The human-in-charge quote in large type.
   - **Four numbered answers**, each with an icon.
   - **A proof strip.** Three big number tiles and a small 1 to 9 readiness ladder with step 6 filled and step 7 outlined.
4. **The system (18%)**, a full-width layered diagram:
   - Row 1, people: five person icons.
   - Row 2, doors: five tiles with logos and status chips.
   - Row 3, the core: a forest box with the seven helpers small in a row, the engine chips, and "Built with" logos.
   - Row 4, inputs and outputs: three panels with logo tiles.

   Arrows run down from the people to the doors to the core, and up from row 4 to the core.
5. **03 Valuable (11%).** Three nested circles (TAM outside, SAM, SOM inside), each with its scope, its count and its yearly value, and the assumption note under them. Beside them, a three-step ladder (Free, Department, Institution) and a calendar chip for the five-year review.
6. **04 Valunique (10%).** The comparison table: the Lyceum column highlighted, symbols in the cells, the legend under it. Then the closing line in large type.
7. **Responsible AI (5%).** Four icon tiles in a row and one line about data sources.
8. **The ask and the footer (6%).**
   - A forest band. Tualang celebrating, with lime growth rings behind it, on the left. The ask line large in the middle, the pilot-measure chips under it, "Every semester adds a ring." in lime. The QR code on a white tile on the right.
   - Under the band, on the mint background, the small footer lines.

## 5. Rules that must not be broken

1. Every word and number exactly as in Section 4. Add no number, claim, statistic, testimonial or logo of your own.
2. No en dash or em dash anywhere. Use commas, colons, periods or the word "to".
3. The status chips say exactly `Built`, `Local, read-only` or `Planned`. Every Planned chip is amber.
4. The UTP mark appears only in the footer, beside the demo line, never in the header or near the title. Lyceum's own logo is never combined with any other logo.
5. Lime is never used for text or lines on a white or mint background.
6. No photographs, no faces, no fictional people.
7. The ownership line and the demo line stay in the footer.
